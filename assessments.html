001 · FAREMETER v0.22.0 · BASE SEPOLIA · 22 SEP 2026 · METHOD DEMONSTRATION

Faremeter v0.22.0

Self-hosted deployment, assessed black-box: probe suites across /verify, /settle, /supported, /accepts; on-chain EIP-712 domain verification; a controlled duplicate-settlement race (N=10 concurrent requests on one funded authorization, three rounds plus control). The race was negative — no double settlement, no delivery without settlement. One low-severity robustness note on inconsistent failure signalling, with no payment-integrity implication. Two hypotheses formed during testing were falsified by evidence and withdrawn; both withdrawals are documented in the report.
