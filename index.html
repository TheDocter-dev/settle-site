Independent measurement for agent-payment infrastructure.

Settle is an independent, protocol-level conformance and measurement lab for the x402 agent-payment protocol. We run funded, single-defect probes against facilitator implementations we deploy ourselves on public testnets — or, with written authorization, against a subject's own staging — and we anchor every claim to a hash-committed artifact before we publish it.

artifact → ledger anchor → OpenTimestamps proof → Bitcoin block. Verify any of it yourself: how to verify.

What we do

  • Conformance assessments of individual x402 facilitator implementations, per pinned version, on public testnets — reproducible from on-chain evidence.
  • Continuous passive observation of x402 settlement activity — on Base today, with further chains under a published scoring method.
  • Pre-registered predictions (from assessment 002), locked and hash-anchored before we probe a subject — confirmations and refutations are both reported.
  • Coordinated disclosure to maintainers under a published policy, with dated public windows.
